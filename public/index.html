How it works

  1. 1
    Upload your CSVYour listings and their competitors. Mark your own with is_client = TRUE and group rivals with competitor_group.
  2. 2
    Choose what to auditPick one client brand, or all of them. Only the chosen brands and their competitors are sent.
  3. 3
    Get scoresEach SKU is scored out of 100 against 29 Amazon content rules, with critical, major and minor issues counted.
  4. 4
    Deep-dive & fixOpen any SKU for every finding with the rule it breaks, a competitor comparison, a compliant rewrite and image checks.
Which columns does the CSV need?

sku_id, is_client (TRUE/FALSE), competitor_group, brand, title, bullet_1–bullet_5, description, image_urls (separated by |). Download the sample to use as a template.
